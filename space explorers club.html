Outer space is the vast, near-perfect vacuum existing beyond Earth's atmosphere (above the \(100\text{ km}\) Kármán line). It contains low-density hydrogen/helium plasma, electromagnetic radiation, and magnetic fields, acting as a container for all matter and energy. Space is not truly empty, featuring "virtual photons" and energy.

Outer space is the near-vacuum expanse beginning about 100 km (the Kármán line) above Earth. It is not completely empty, containing low-density hydrogen, plasma, and radiation.
