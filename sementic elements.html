Facts about dogs

Dogs are highly intelligent, social animals domesticated over 20,000 years ago from an extinct wolf species. They possess an extraordinary sense of smell up to 100,000 times more sensitive than humans, and can understand over 100 words or gestures. Key facts include that they are left or right-pawed, have unique nose prints, and sweat through their paws.
