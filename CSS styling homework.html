Harry Potter is the story of a young orphan who discovers on his eleventh birthday that he is a wizard and has been accepted into Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. Created by J.K. Rowling, the series follows Harry and his best friends, Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger, as they navigate the complexities of magic, teenage life, and a growing darkness. The overarching plot centers on Harry's struggle against Lord Voldemort, a dark wizard intent on becoming immortal and conquering the wizarding world.

Beyond the books, the franchise has expanded into a massive cultural phenomenon known as the Wizarding World. This includes a blockbuster eight-film series, the Fantastic Beasts prequels, and the hit stage play The Cursed Child. Today, fans continue to engage with the lore through immersive experiences like The Wizarding World of Harry Potter theme parks and the video game Hogwarts Legacy. With a new HBO television series currently in production, the story is set to be reintroduced to a whole new generation.
