Cats

Cats make wonderful companions because they are generally low-maintenance, independent animals that are easy to house-train and keep clean. Furthermore, their affectionate purring and playful nature provide significant stress relief and emotional support, promoting a calmer and healthier home environment.

Cats can be destructive pets that scratch furniture, shed excessively, and require constant cleaning of their smelly litter boxes. Furthermore, they are often aloof, selfish, and can destroy local ecosystems by killing billions of birds and small mammals annually.
