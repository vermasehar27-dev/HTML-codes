What is tarot?

Tarot is a deck of 78 cards featuring symbolic imagery, originally created in 15th-century Italy as a trick-taking game. Today, it is primarily used for introspection, guidance, and divination, with practitioners interpreting cards to gain insights into past, present, or future situations.
